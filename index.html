FAQ

Things you might want to know.

Can I request something that isn't listed?

Yes! Custom requests are welcome. Send us your idea through the order form.

Can I choose the colour?

Usually, yes. Available colours depend on the filament we have at the time.

Can I choose the size?

For many prints we can change the size. Larger prints may cost more because they use more material and take longer.

How long does a print take?

It depends on the model and size. We'll let you know an estimated print time before confirming the order.

Do you sell finished prints?

Yes! Check the popular prints section, or request something custom.

What if I have an STL file?

Send the details through the order form. We can check whether the model is suitable for printing.
