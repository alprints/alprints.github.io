FAQ

Got questions?

Can I request something that isn't listed?

Yes! Custom requests are welcome. Tell us what you want using the order form.

Can I choose the colour?

Usually, yes. Available colours depend on the filament we have available.

Can I choose the size?

For many prints we can change the size. Larger prints may cost more.

How long does a print take?

It depends on the model and size. We'll provide an estimated print time.

What if I have an STL file?

That's fine! Tell us about it in the order form and we'll check whether it's suitable for printing.
